## Customer-Sales-Revenue-Analytics-Dashboard 


### 📊 Project Overview

The "Customer Sales & Revenue Analytics Dashboard" is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, regional performance, returns, and loss-making transactions.

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview of business performance along with a detailed customer and product analysis page.

### 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor overall sales and profit performance
* Analyze sales trends across years
* Compare sales performance across regions and categories
* Identify top-performing customers and products
* Analyze profitability across discount bands
* Identify loss-making products and transactions
* Analyze return rates by product category
* Provide interactive filtering for business analysis

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Microsoft Excel
* Data Visualization
* Data Cleaning & Transformation
* Exploratory Data Analysis

## 📁 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Sales Dashboard

Provides a high-level overview of business performance, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Total Products
* Total Customers
* Total Orders
* Loss-Making Transactions
* Sales and Profit by Year
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Discount Band
* Return Rate by Category

### 2. Customer & Product Analysis

Provides detailed analysis of customer and product performance, including:

* Top 10 Products by Profit
* Top 10 Customers by Profit
* Customer Sales vs Profit
* Loss-Making Products
* Sales and Profit KPIs
* Interactive Year, Region, and Category filters

## 📈 Key Insights

The dashboard can be used to identify:

* Overall sales and profitability trends
* Best-performing regions and product categories
* Highest-profit customers and products
* Products generating negative profit
* Relationship between customer sales and profit
* Categories with comparatively higher return rates
* Impact of discount levels on profitability

## 🎛️ Interactive Features

The dashboard includes interactive filters for:

* Year
* Region
* Category

Selecting a filter dynamically updates the KPIs and visualizations across the dashboard.
